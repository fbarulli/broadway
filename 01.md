I would **not modify `AnalysisPlan` yet**. It is currently a generic statistical-result container, while this new layer wants a more explicit structure:

**Question → Evidence → Ramification**

The first small step should be a new typed result, probably in `stats/diagnostics.py` or a new `stats/diagnostic_models.py`:

```python
class DiagnosticResult(BaseModel):
    question: str
    evidence: list[str]
    ramification: str
    warnings: list[str] = []
```

Then diagnostic #1 can produce something conceptually like:

```text
question:
"Is the mean relationship correctly specified?"

evidence:
- residual-vs-fitted plot persisted

ramification:
"Systematic residual structure suggests the mean function may be misspecified;
consider an appropriate transformation, nonlinear term, spline, or interaction,
then refit and re-diagnose."
```

For the walkthrough/router architecture, I would **split these plots by diagnostic question** rather than keep one omnibus `plot_residuals()` artifact.
