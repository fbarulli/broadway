## Final Framework

**Trust the data → learn from it → establish causality → establish trust → make decisions → communicate and operationalize**

---

### 1. Foundations
*Is the data trustworthy and well-understood?*
- Data quality
- Missing data
- Data privacy & governance
- Measurement & metrics
- Exploratory analysis
- Feature engineering

### 2. Modeling & Inference
*What can we learn or predict from the data?*
- Data representation & modeling *(dimensional/relational structure — distinct from statistical modeling below)*
- Supervised learning
- Unsupervised learning
- Statistical inference
- Time-series analysis
- Model selection
- Model validation

### 3. Experimentation & Causal Inference
*What actually caused what, and how do we know?*
- Experimentation
- Causal inference
- Power & experiment design
- Sequential monitoring
- Multiple testing
- Heterogeneous treatment effects

### 4. Trust & Robustness
*Can we rely on the conclusions in the real world?*
- Robustness & sensitivity analysis
- Leakage & bias
- Uncertainty quantification
- Model interpretability
- Distribution & shift analysis
- Fairness & subgroup risk

### 5. Decisioning & Impact
*So what — what should the business do?*
- Decision layer
- Optimization
- Cost-benefit / ROI translation

### 6. Production & Communication
*How does this reach stakeholders and stay alive?*
- Data storytelling & visualization
- Reproducibility
- Deployment / productionization
- Monitoring
